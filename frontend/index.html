Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about requesting documents.

Requesting Documents

What documents can I request?

You can request the Transcript of Records (₱250), Honorable Dismissal (₱350), Diploma (₱500), Certified True Copy of Academic Record (₱50), and these ₱100 documents:

  • Certificate of Enrollment (COE)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Certification of Grades (COG)
  • Certificate of No Pending Requirement
  • Certificate of Registration (COR)
  • Certificate of Graduation
  • Certificate of Units Earned
  • Certificate of General Weighted Average (GWA)
  • Internship/Training Certification
  • NSTP/CWTS-related Certification
  • Other Certifications

See the Available Documents on the Home page for the full list.

How do I request a document?

Log in to your student account, open the Document Catalog, and choose the document. Fill out the form, upload the files it asks for, choose how you will pay and how you will receive it, then submit. You will get a reference number (your Request ID) and a QR code for your request.

What requirements do I need?

It depends on the document:

  • Student verification (most certificates): the system checks your student record. You only upload the files it asks for.
  • Accomplished request form (Transcript of Records and Honorable Dismissal).
  • Certified True Copy: the record to be certified, or a signed request form.
  • Diploma: bring the physical payment receipt when you claim it.

Click a document on the Home page to see its exact requirements. Every file you upload is checked: it must be readable, complete, the right document, and show your name.

Request Status

How can I track my request?

Use the Track page: enter your reference number from your request slip or QR code. You can also log in and open My Requests to see all your requests and their status.

What do the request statuses mean?
  • Pending: your request was received.
  • Under Verification: your documents and payment are being checked.
  • Needs Correction: something must be fixed. Open the request, read the reason, and upload the correct files.
  • Approved: your document is being prepared or is ready for release.
  • Completed: your document was released.
  • Rejected: the request was not approved. See the remarks for the reason.
How long does processing take?

Most documents are processed on the same business day. The Transcript of Records and Diploma take 5–7 working days, and the Certificate of GWA takes 1–3 working days. The expected date is shown on your request.

Release & Claiming

How will I know if my document is ready?

You will get a notification in your account, and the status of your request changes to Approved (Ready for Release). Soft copies (for example COG and COR) can be downloaded from your dashboard once they are ready.

How can I claim my document?

Go to the Registrar's Office on your claim date, between 9:00 A.M. and 4:00 P.M. only. Bring a valid ID and the hard copy of your payment receipt. Your request is marked Completed once it is released.

Can someone else claim it for me?

Yes. Your representative must bring your ID and an authorization letter from you.

Problems & Support

What if I entered incorrect information?

If the system or the Registrar finds a problem, your request becomes Needs Correction. Open it from My Requests, read what to fix, and upload the corrected files. If you already submitted and noticed a mistake yourself, contact the Registrar's Office as soon as possible, before your document is released.

What if I lost my Request ID?

Log in and open My Requests. Your tracking number is listed beside every request, and the QR code and notifications also show it. If you cannot log in, visit the Registrar's Office with a valid ID.

What if my request is rejected?

Open the request and read the remarks to see the reason. You can submit a new request once the rejected one is closed. If you already paid, contact the Registrar's Office to discuss the payment.
